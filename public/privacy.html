Privacy Policy

What the website collects when you ask us to call you back, why we need it, how long we keep it, and how to have it deleted.

Effective and last updated October 28, 2026

Who we are

This website belongs to Vernon Auto Repair, a family-owned auto repair shop at 38-01 Vernon Blvd, Long Island City, NY 11101, United States. You can reach us any time at +1 (718) 666-7592. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Vernon Auto Repair.

What the callback form collects

When you send a callback request, we receive only what you type into the form:

  • Your name, so we know who we’re calling.
  • Your phone number, so we can call you back.
  • Your car’s make and model, so we can prepare.
  • A short description of the problem, so we can give you useful advice on the call.

Please don’t include anything sensitive in the description, such as payment card numbers or health information. We don’t need it.

Information collected automatically

  • Security checks. When the form is submitted, our server sees your IP address. We use it only to stop abuse (no more than five requests per hour from one connection). We store a scrambled, one-way version of it, never the address itself, and it’s deleted automatically after about an hour.
  • Spam protection. The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a person, not a bot, is sending it. Turnstile looks at technical signals from your browser for this check only. See Cloudflare’s Turnstile privacy addendum.
  • Hosting logs. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and protect the site.
  • Visit statistics, only if you allow them. If you turn on visit statistics, we use Plausible Analytics to count page views. Plausible doesn’t use cookies, doesn’t collect personal data and doesn’t track you across other sites.

How we use it

We use what you send for one purpose: to call you back about your car and, if you choose, to arrange a repair, tow or inspection. We don’t use it for marketing, we don’t add you to a mailing list, and we don’t sell it.

How long we keep it

The website itself doesn’t store your request in a database. When you press “Request callback,” your details are sent straight to the shop’s email inbox and nowhere else.

  • We delete callback emails within 90 days of handling your request.
  • If the call turns into a repair job, the details you gave become part of the usual work record for your car, kept for as long as we need it for that job, warranty questions and our legal obligations.
  • Security data (the scrambled IP value) expires automatically after about an hour.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We use a small number of service providers who handle data only on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare, for website hosting, security and the Turnstile spam check.
  • Resend, to deliver the callback request to our inbox by email.
  • Google, only if you choose to load the map on our Visit section.
  • Plausible Analytics, only if you turn on visit statistics.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

Whatever state you live in, you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it.

How to ask us to delete your information

  • Call us at +1 (718) 666-7592, any time, or
  • Visit or write to us at 38-01 Vernon Blvd, Long Island City, NY 11101.

To make sure we delete the right request, we’ll ask for the name and phone number you used on the form. We’ll confirm once it’s done, and we aim to respond within 45 days. Someone you’ve authorized can make the request for you.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We don’t sell or share personal information, and we don’t use sensitive personal information. We won’t treat you differently for using any of these rights. Use the contact methods above to make a request. In the last 12 months, the only categories of personal information collected through this website are identifiers (name, phone number, IP address), the vehicle and problem details you describe, and basic technical data used for security.

New York residents

We protect your information with reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, as New York’s SHIELD Act requires. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS) for the whole site, keeping passwords and API keys off the public website, limiting who can read the shop inbox, and deleting requests when we no longer need them. If a security breach ever affects your private information, we’ll notify you as New York law requires.

Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as an opt-out: visit statistics stay off, even if you click “Accept all.”

Security

The whole site runs over HTTPS. The form checks every request on our server, blocks automated spam, and limits how often it can be used. No customer details are stored on the website.

If we change how we handle your information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If the change affects cookies, we’ll ask for your choices again.
